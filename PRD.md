Product Requirements Document (PRD)

Creator CRM & Categorization Framework

Product: Creator Relationship Management System (CRMS)

Version: 1.0

⸻

1. Objective

Build a centralized Creator CRM that enables the brand to:

* Store all creator information in one place.
* Score creators objectively.
* Categorize creators into the appropriate collaboration model.
* Track outreach and negotiations.
* Measure ROI from every creator partnership.
* Build long-term ambassador relationships instead of one-off influencer campaigns.

⸻

2. Business Goals

* Create a database of 500+ creators.
* Reduce time spent evaluating creators.
* Standardize creator selection.
* Improve ROI on creator marketing.
* Build a high-quality ambassador network.
